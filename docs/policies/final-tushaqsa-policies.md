# REFUND, RETURN, REPLACEMENT & CANCELLATION POLICY

**Tushaqsa Homedecor**  
*An initiative of Aadya Society for Vocational Education & Life Skill Programmes*

Thank you for shopping with Tushaqsa Homedecor. We value your trust and appreciate your support for handmade, artisan-crafted and thoughtfully curated products.

We take great care in selecting, preparing, packaging and delivering our products. However, we understand that occasional issues may arise. Please read our policy carefully before placing an order.

## 1. Return & Replacement Policy

At Tushaqsa Homedecor, we aim to deliver products in excellent condition. Returns and replacements are accepted under the following circumstances:

- You receive a damaged or defective product.
- You receive a product different from the one you ordered.
- The product has a manufacturing defect.

Customers must report any such issue within **24 hours of receiving the order**.

**Important:** An unboxing video showing the original parcel opening and the reported damage or defect is mandatory for processing a claim.

## 2. Conditions for Return & Replacement

To be eligible for a return or replacement, the following conditions must be fulfilled:

1. The product must be unused, unwashed and in its original condition.
2. Original packaging, tags, labels and accessories must be intact.
3. The customer must provide the order ID, product photographs and an unboxing video wherever applicable.
4. Products that have been used, altered, washed or damaged by the customer will not be eligible for return or replacement.
5. Replacement will be subject to product availability.

## 3. Products Not Eligible for Return or Replacement

The following products will not be eligible for return or exchange:

- Customized or personalised orders.
- Products purchased during sale periods or promotional offers.
- Products purchased using discount coupons or promotional discount codes.
- International orders, except where applicable consumer rights require otherwise.
- Products showing signs of use, alteration or customer-caused damage.
- Products returned without original packaging, tags and labels.

Please note that differences in personal preference, style, design, colour perception or print are not considered manufacturing defects.

## 4. Return Shipping & Pickup Policy

**For damaged, defective or incorrect products:**

If your claim is approved, Tushaqsa Homedecor will arrange a pickup of the product at no additional cost to you.

**For returns due to personal reasons:**

For return requests arising from personal preferences or a change of mind, customers must arrange and bear the return shipping costs themselves. Such returns are subject to prior approval and the applicable eligibility conditions.

Customers must share the courier name and tracking details with us once the product has been shipped.

## 5. How to Request a Return or Replacement

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please follow these steps:

1. Contact our customer support within 24 hours of delivery.
2. Mention your order ID and product name.
3. Provide clear photographs of the product and its packaging.
4. Share the parcel opening/unboxing video showing the issue.
5. Our team will review your request and communicate the next steps.

Once the request is approved, we will arrange a pickup or provide the return shipping instructions.

Please do not ship any product back without receiving confirmation from our team.

## 6. Order Cancellation Policy

We understand that customers may occasionally need to cancel an order.

- Cancellation requests must be submitted within 24 hours of placing the order.
- If the order is cancelled before dispatch, the full amount paid will be refunded.
- Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.
- After delivery, replacement or refund requests will be considered only under the applicable defective, damaged or incorrect-product conditions of this policy.

Tushaqsa Homedecor reserves the right to cancel an order in case of unforeseen circumstances, stock unavailability or other operational issues. In such cases, any amount paid will be refunded.

## 7. Refund Policy

Refunds will be considered in the following situations:

- An eligible order is cancelled before dispatch.
- A product is received damaged, defective or incorrect and a refund is approved by our team.
- An approved return is received and passes our quality inspection.

Once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund will be processed to the original mode of payment.

**Refund Processing Time:** Approved refunds will generally be processed within 7–10 working days, subject to payment gateway and banking processing timelines.

Please note that refunds for returned products will only be initiated after the product has been received and inspected by our team.

## 8. Damaged Package at the Time of Delivery

If you notice that the outer packaging is visibly damaged, tampered with or opened at the time of delivery, we recommend refusing the delivery and informing our customer support immediately.

If you accept the package, please record an unboxing video before opening or removing the product from its packaging.

This will help us investigate and resolve your complaint efficiently.

## 9. Important Information About Handmade Products

Many of our products are handmade or artisan-crafted. Slight variations in colour, texture, patterns, finish and dimensions may occur due to the nature of handcrafted production.

Such minor variations are part of the uniqueness and character of handmade products and shall not automatically be considered manufacturing defects.

We encourage customers to review product descriptions, images, dimensions and specifications carefully before placing an order.
